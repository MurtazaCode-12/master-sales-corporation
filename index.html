Corporate Origin & Leadership

Grounded Engineering.
Dedicated Wholesale Scale.

Founded in 2021, Master Sales Corporation was established in Pune by two dedicated partners—Hussain Vohra and Hussaini Diwan—combining modern digital commercial systems with over three decades of engineering and manufacturing domain expertise.

Located centrally in Uruli Devachi, MSC operates as a structured B2B distribution channel connecting premier sanitaryware and plumbing manufacturers with hardware stores, retail merchants, and real estate developers across Maharashtra, Goa, and Karnataka.
